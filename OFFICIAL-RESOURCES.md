# Official AQA resources

Links to the official materials AQA makes available on its site: past papers,
mark schemes, inserts, sample assessment materials and examiner reports.
Availability varies; some materials may be unavailable or withheld. Nothing on
this page is copied from AQA; these are links to AQA's own pages.

## GCSE English Language (8700)

- Subject page: https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-language-8700
- Assessment resources (past papers, mark schemes, inserts, sample sets):
  https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-language-8700/assessment-resources
- Specification at a glance is linked from the subject page.

Notes:
- The 2026 sample assessment set (Paper 1 Q1 multiple-choice format, revised
  stems) is listed under the same assessment-resources page as "Sample set".
- New series papers are usually added to the same page after each exam series,
  though availability and timing can vary.

## Using the official materials

- Question papers list the printed mark total per question in brackets.
- Mark schemes give the indicative content and level descriptors examiners
  use; the level wording is AQA's. Any scores in the model answers in this
  repository are estimates from applying those descriptors, not official
  grades.
- Examiner reports (same page, after each series) describe how candidates
  actually performed on each question.
