# GCSE English (AQA) practice model answers

Original model answers and companion commentary for AQA GCSE English Language (8700) and English Literature (8702). Each file has a full original answer plus the author's own notes. They are not examiner-marked, and no mark is guaranteed. Comments on level are the author's own reading, not an AQA result. The question wording is not reproduced: read each answer next to the official paper, linked in SOURCES.md.

## What is here

- `models/`: thirty files (v11: Q4 and Q23 also revised, Q4 paraphrasing a free script sample with no verbatim lines; v10: Q19 and Q22 revised with short quotations from publisher or broadcaster chapter-one extracts, named in each file). The 8702 Paper 2 files for Blood Brothers Q4, Anita and Me Q22 and Pigeon English Q23 are labelled "provisional study response" in their titles. They are not completed high-level models.
  - 8700 Paper 1 and Paper 2, June 2025

  - 8702 Paper 2, June 2025: My Name Is Leon Q19 (model), Blood Brothers Q4, Anita and Me Q22 and Pigeon English Q23 (provisional responses), An Inspector Calls Q2, Animal Farm Q18, Princess & The Hustler Q6, A Taste of Honey Q11, Leave Taking Q10, DNA Q7, Lord of the Flies Q13, Telling Tales Q15 ('Odour of Chrysanthemums' with 'Chemistry'), poetry Q25, Q26 ('Exposure' with 'The Charge of the Light Brigade'), Q27 ('In a London Drawingroom' with 'Lines Written in Early Spring'), Q28.1 and Q28.2

## What is not here

AQA question papers, inserts and mark schemes are AQA's material, and many extracts and poems are still in copyright. None of them are in this repo. Each model refers to the question by series, paper and number, so open the linked AQA file to see the wording.

## Limits

- Literature answers are sample options, not selected texts.
- Quotations: modern copyrighted texts are not quoted beyond single words. Where the analysis points to a phrase, it gives a paraphrase and a location: "line N" is the printed line number of the AQA insert, poem line numbers are the printed lines in the question paper, the play is cited by act, and 'Table' by section of the Children's Poetry Archive page. Public-domain texts, as far as UK copyright goes (Shakespeare, Stevenson, Jefferies, Kemble, Dickens, Austen, Owen, Tennyson, Orwell, Lawrence, Eliot, Wordsworth) are quoted with attribution. Each file's own creative writing is original.
- Exception: the Leave Taking (Q10), DNA (Q7), Lord of the Flies (Q13), Princess & The Hustler (Q6) and A Taste of Honey (Q11) models contain short quotations from the in-copyright play or novel. They are taken only from the study guides and theatre packs named in SOURCES.md (Nick Hern Books, Pearson, Faber, National Theatre) and each file separates quoted play dialogue from quoted guide commentary, were not checked against the playscript or novel, and carry page numbers where the guide gave them. They are sample options built from secondary sources, not selected texts.
- Provisional study responses (Blood Brothers Q4, Anita and Me Q22, Pigeon English Q23) were written without the full text, from exam-board teaching resources, BBC pages or a commercial revision site named in each file and in SOURCES.md. Each says what its sources are, uses only the quotations those sources print, and says it could not check them against the play or novel. Read them as study material and check every point against your own copy.
- Coverage is sampled: 27 model answers and 3 provisional study responses (30 Literature questions in all), and the Language papers listed above. This is not a full set of answers for every past paper.
